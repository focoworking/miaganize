Specs, terms & visibility

The details.

Creative specs

  • Logo: SVG or PNG, transparent, min. 1000 px wide
  • Newsletter copy: up to 60 words + 1 URL (UTM welcome)
  • Images we receive: JPG/WebP, min. 3000 px long side, with usage rights
  • Branded Story: we produce all photography; you approve facts, not editorial voice

Booking terms

  • Payment by secure link (Stripe) to confirm the date
  • Materials due 7 days before publication
  • Full refund if cancelled 7+ days before publication
  • Founding Partner: pay upfront or in 4 quarterly installments
  • All paid content is labeled “Partner” (FTC Endorsement Guides)
  • Founding rates valid for contracts signed before Feb 28, 2027

Visibility beyond social

  • SEO: every Partner story is indexed with structured data (schema.org)
  • AEO: answers formatted for Google AI Overviews and featured snippets
  • GEO / LLM: sourced, dated content built to be cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini
  • Bilingual pages double your search footprint (EN + ES)
