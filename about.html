About

A magazine made by its photographers.

What is MIAGAZINE? MIAGAZINE is a bilingual digital luxury magazine about Miami, founded in 2026. It publishes photographic editorials and a researched Journal on fashion, real estate, economy, music, design, food, tech and waterfront living, created with a curated network of Miami photographers, plus a free weekly newsletter called The Lens.

Editorial standards

  • Every story is curated and edited by our editorial team.
  • Paid content is always labeled “Partner”.
  • Every image is credited to its photographer and team.
  • Real photography only. AI may assist with translation and metadata, always reviewed by an editor. Speculative pieces are clearly labeled as fiction.
  • Journal articles cite their sources and show the date they were last updated.
