About

A magazine made by its photographers.

What is MIAGAZINE? MIAGAZINE is a digital luxury magazine about Miami, founded in 2026. It publishes photographic editorials on fashion, architecture, hospitality, art and waterfront living, created by a curated network of Miami photographers, plus a free weekly newsletter called The Lens.

Editorial standards

  • Every story is curated and edited by our editorial team.
  • Paid content is always labeled “Partner”.
  • Every image is credited to its photographer and creative team.
  • Real photography only. We do not publish AI-generated imagery as editorial. AI tools may assist with translation and metadata, always reviewed by an editor.
  • Guides are dated and updated; corrections are noted at the end of the article.
