## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you give us

- **Newsletter.** Your email address and, if you choose to provide them, your name and language preference.
- **Editorial submissions.** Your name, email, website or social handle, biography, the photographs or other works you submit, captions, location and date details, and the **model and property releases** you attach. Releases may contain the names, signatures and contact details of the people photographed and, for minors, of their parent or legal guardian.
- **Careers applications.** Your name, contact details, résumé, portfolio, links, cover letter and anything else you choose to include. Please do not send us government ID numbers, health information or other sensitive data unless we specifically ask for it and the law allows it.
- **Partners and advertisers.** Your name, business name, title, email, phone, billing address, tax information where required, and campaign materials. Payments are processed by Stripe through Stripe Payment Links. **We do not receive or store your full card number or security code**; we only see limited information such as the card brand, the last four digits, the amount and the payment status.
- **Communications.** Anything you send to us by email, contact form, direct message or survey, including corrections requests and tips.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

- **Analytics.** We use privacy-focused, cookieless web analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or Plausible) that measure aggregated data like pages visited, referring site, approximate country, device type and browser. They are designed not to identify you or follow you across sites.
- **Technical and security data.** Our hosting and content delivery providers process IP addresses, request logs and similar data to deliver pages, prevent abuse and keep the Service secure.
- **Cookies and local storage.** We store your cookie choice and language preference in your browser. Marketing pixels (for example, from Meta or Google) only load if you give consent. See our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies.html).
- **Email engagement.** Our newsletter platform may record whether an email was delivered or opened and which links were clicked, so we can understand what readers find useful and keep our list healthy.

### 2.3 Information from other sources

- **Social media.** If you interact with our accounts, the platform may share your public handle and the content of your message or comment with us.
- **Contributors and partners.** A photographer may give us a release that includes your information, or a partner company may give us your work contact details.
- **Events.** We may photograph people at public events (see Section 10).

## 3. How we use information and our legal bases

For visitors in the EU/EEA and the UK, we rely on the legal bases in Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation ([Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)) and the UK GDPR.

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Send *The Lens* newsletter | Email, preferences, engagement | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Review, edit, publish and credit submissions | Submission data, works, releases | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Evaluate job applications | Application data | Steps prior to a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interests |
| Sell and deliver partner campaigns, invoice and collect | Partner and billing data | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| Answer messages, corrections and requests | Communications | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Measure audience in aggregate | Cookieless analytics | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Marketing pixels and ad measurement | Cookie identifiers, events | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Security, fraud prevention, legal claims | Logs, records | Legitimate interests and legal obligation |
| Journalism and coverage of public events | Images, names in public context | Legitimate interests and the journalistic exemptions provided by applicable law |

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal. Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object (see Section 9).

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

**We do not sell your personal information**, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising unless you have opted in to marketing cookies. We share information only:

- with the service providers described in Section 4;
- when we publish your work, with the credit you agreed to;

- when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, safety and property of MIAGAZINE, our readers or others;
- in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to this Policy; or
- with your consent or at your direction.

## 6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described, then delete or anonymize it.

| Category | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Newsletter subscription | Until you unsubscribe, plus up to 30 days. We keep a suppression record of your email so we don't email you again. |
| Accepted submissions and releases | For as long as the work is published or licensed, plus 5 years to defend legal claims |
| Declined submissions | Up to 12 months after our decision |
| Careers applications | Up to 24 months after the process ends, unless you ask us to delete sooner; if hired, per employment records rules |

We may keep information longer when the law requires it or to resolve disputes.

## 9. Your privacy rights

### 9.1 Everyone

Regardless of where you live, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, unsubscribe from our newsletter, and withdraw any consent. We will honor reasonable requests unless we must keep the information for legal, security or journalistic reasons.

### 9.2 Florida residents

The Florida Digital Bill of Rights ([Fla. Stat. §501.701 et seq.](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2024/501.701)) gives Florida consumers rights to confirm, access, correct, delete and obtain a copy of their personal data, and to opt out of its sale, targeted advertising and certain profiling. The law applies only to large "controllers" that meet specific thresholds, including more than US$1 billion in global gross annual revenue. MIAGAZINE does not currently meet those thresholds, but **we voluntarily honor similar requests** from Florida residents.

### 9.3 California residents

The California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ([Cal. Civ. Code §1798.100 et seq.](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa)), applies to businesses that meet certain thresholds, such as annual gross revenue above the amount set by law (currently adjusted for inflation from US$25 million) or buying, selling or sharing the personal information of 100,000 or more consumers or households. If and when the CCPA applies to us, California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and disclose it; to access, delete and correct it; to opt out of its sale or sharing; to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent. We honor these requests voluntarily in the meantime.

The categories we collected in the last 12 months are described in Section 2 (identifiers, commercial, internet activity, professional and audio/visual information). We do not sell personal information. Marketing pixels, which run only with your consent, may count as "sharing" under the CCPA; you can refuse them at any time.

**Shine the Light.** Under Cal. Civ. Code §1798.83, California residents may ask whether we disclosed personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes in the prior calendar year. We do not make such disclosures.

### 9.4 EU/EEA and UK residents

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to: access your data (Art. 15); rectification (Art. 16); erasure (Art. 17); restriction of processing (Art. 18); data portability (Art. 20); object to processing based on legitimate interests, and at any time to direct marketing (Art. 21); and withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (Art. 77) or, in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

### 9.5 Global Privacy Control

We honor the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** browser signal. When we detect it, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising, and we do not load marketing pixels for that browser. California regulations recognize GPC as a valid opt-out preference signal (Cal. Code Regs. tit. 11, §7025).

## 10. Photographs of people and events

MIAGAZINE documents public life in Miami. Our photographers and contributors may photograph people attending public events or in public spaces as part of editorial coverage. We use such images for news, commentary and editorial purposes, not to endorse products. We will not use a person's name or likeness to advertise a product or service without the consent required by Florida law ([Fla. Stat. §540.08](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2024/540.08)).

We only publish identifiable images of minors with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, except incidental images in crowds at public news events, which we remove on a parent's or guardian's request.

**Removal requests.** If you appear in an image on our site or social media and would like it removed, blurred or uncredited, email privacy@miagazine.com with the link to the image and a short description of how to identify you. We review every request in good faith and respond within 15 business days. We generally grant requests involving minors, private settings, safety concerns or images that are not newsworthy; we may decline when there is a clear public interest in the image.

## 11. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ([15 U.S.C. §6501 et seq.](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6501&num=0&edition=prelim)) and the FTC's COPPA Rule ([16 CFR Part 312](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312)). If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, write to privacy@miagazine.com and we will delete it.

## 12. Newsletter and email

We send *The Lens* only to people who subscribe. Every email includes our mailing address and a working unsubscribe link, and we process unsubscribes promptly, as required by the CAN-SPAM Act ([15 U.S.C. §7701 et seq.](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7701&num=0&edition=prelim); see the [FTC's compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)). Sponsored content in the newsletter is labeled "Partner." Even after you unsubscribe, we may send transactional messages, for example about a submission or invoice.

## 13. How to exercise your rights

Email **privacy@miagazine.com** with the subject "Privacy request," tell us what you are asking for, and include the email address you used with us. To protect you, we may ask for information to verify your identity before acting on a request; we will only use it for verification. Authorized agents must provide proof of authorization.

We will acknowledge your request within 10 business days and respond within **45 days** (U.S. requests) or **one month** (EU/UK requests). We may extend these periods where the law allows and will tell you if we do. Requests are free, unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive. If we deny a request, we will explain why and, where applicable, how to appeal; to appeal, reply to our decision with the subject "Privacy appeal."
