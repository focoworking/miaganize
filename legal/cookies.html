2. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. They let the site remember information between pages or visits, such as a preference or whether you are signed in. Cookies set by the site you are visiting are "first-party"; cookies set by another domain (for example, a social network whose content is embedded on the page) are "third-party." Some cookies expire when you close the browser ("session cookies"); others remain for a set time ("persistent cookies").

Local storage (localStorage) is a space in your browser where a site can save small pieces of data. Unlike cookies, it is not sent to the server with every request, and it stays until it is deleted by the site or by you.

Similar technologies include pixels (tiny images or scripts that report that a page was viewed or an action was taken) and software development kits. In this Policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

3. Our approach

We designed the Site to work with as little tracking as possible:

  • Nothing beyond what is strictly necessary runs until you make a choice in the banner.
  • Our default analytics are cookieless and do not build individual profiles.
  • Marketing tags are off by default. They only load if MIAGAZINE has enabled them for a campaign and you have consented to the Marketing category.
  • Embedded third-party content (Instagram, YouTube and similar) is loaded only after consent or when you click to load it.
  • Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and you can change your choice at any time.

4. Categories we use

4.1 Necessary

These are required for the Site to work and to remember the choices you make. They cannot be switched off in our panel, because without them we could not remember that you rejected other cookies. We use:

  • mg-consent (local storage, first-party): stores your cookie choice (which categories you accepted or rejected) and when you made it, so we don't ask again on every page.
  • Language preference (local storage or cookie, first-party): remembers whether you prefer English or Spanish.
  • Security and delivery: our hosting and content delivery provider may set strictly necessary cookies to protect the Site from bots and abuse and to balance traffic.

4.2 Analytics

We measure audience with privacy-focused, cookieless tools such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or Plausible. They count page views, referrers, approximate country and device type in aggregate, without storing cookies on your device or identifying you across sites.

If we ever use an analytics tool that sets cookies or similar identifiers, we will do so only with your consent to the Analytics category, and we will list it in the table below.

4.3 Marketing

Marketing tags help us measure the results of campaigns and show relevant ads on other platforms, for example the Meta Pixel or Google advertising tags. They may set cookies that identify your browser across sites and share data with those companies under their own privacy policies.

These tags are disabled by default. They load only if (a) we have enabled them and (b) you have accepted the Marketing category. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we will not load them.

4.4 Embedded third-party content

Some articles include posts, videos or maps from third parties such as Instagram or YouTube. These services may set their own cookies and collect data when the content loads. To protect you, we show a placeholder and load the embed only after you have given consent or click to load that specific item. Where available, we use privacy-enhanced modes (such as YouTube's youtube-nocookie.com domain).

6. How to change your preferences

  • On our Site. Click "Cookie preferences" in the footer of any page to reopen the panel. You can accept or reject each category and save. Withdrawing consent stops non-essential tags from loading from then on; to remove cookies already set, also clear them in your browser.
  • In your browser. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and site data, and some block third-party cookies by default. Instructions are in your browser's help section (for example, Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge). If you clear site data, we will no longer remember your choice and will show the banner again.
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC). If your browser or extension sends the GPC signal, we treat it as a refusal of marketing cookies and as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Learn more in our Privacy Policy.
  • Platform settings. You can also manage ad preferences directly in your Meta and Google accounts.

7. How long your consent lasts

We keep your choice for 12 months. After that, or sooner if we make material changes to the tools we use, we will ask you again. You can change it at any time through the footer link.

8. Legal framework

  • European Union and EEA. Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC, as amended by Directive 2009/136/EC) requires consent before storing or accessing information on a user's device, unless it is strictly necessary to provide a service the user requested. When cookies involve personal data, the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) also applies, including its standard for valid consent (freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous; Articles 4(11) and 7).
  • United Kingdom. Similar rules apply under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations and the UK GDPR.
  • United States. Where state privacy laws apply, marketing cookies can count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising," which you can opt out of through our panel or with GPC. See our Privacy Policy for state-specific rights.
