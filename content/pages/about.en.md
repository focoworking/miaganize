!! What we are: a bilingual digital luxury magazine, born in Miami in 2026.
!! Who makes it: publicists, reporters, photographers, filmmakers and content creators.
!! What we cover: Miami's luxury, events, nightlife, art, fashion, real estate and public life.
!! Why now: the city changed faster than the media that tells its story.

## Miami deserves a magazine that matches it

Miami is no longer just a destination. It hosts global art fairs, attracts financial capital, defines Latin music and dresses for the night like few cities on earth. Yet anyone who wants to follow it usually finds the same two options: expensive, slow print titles, or fast social feeds with no point of view.

**MIAGAZINE was born in response to that demand.** We are an emerging initiative created because the public asked for something specific: to see Miami with good taste, in real time, with verified information and through the eyes of people who live the city from the inside.

> Miami, through the lens. That simple, that demanding.

## What is a digital magazine?

A digital magazine is an editorial publication designed, published and distributed through digital media: website, newsletter, social channels, video and downloadable editions. It keeps the best of print (curation, design, photography, an editorial voice) and adds what paper can't:

| Traditional print magazine | MIAGAZINE (digital magazine) |
|---|---|
| Comes out monthly or bimonthly | Publishes weekly and covers events in real time |
| Reaches those who buy or receive it | Reaches anyone, free, on any screen |
| Photos and text only | Photo, video, audio, interactive guides and newsletter |
| One language | English and Spanish |
| Hard to measure | Clear metrics for readers and brands |
| High printing and distribution costs | Near-free distribution: web, email, social and community |

We also produce **quarterly special editions**, downloadable and available as print-on-demand, for those who love holding a magazine.

## A collective, not a traditional newsroom

MIAGAZINE works as a **select group** of independent professionals who share one vision:

- **Publicists and strategists** who connect brands with stories people actually want to see.
- **Reporters and writers** who research, verify and write in English and Spanish.
- **Photographers and filmmakers** who turn every event, street and sunset into a cover.
- **Content creators** who take the magazine to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest.
- **Curators and editors** who make sure everything we publish has standards, sources and style.

Every piece is curated. Every image is credited. Every fact has a source.

## What we cover

- **Luxury and lifestyle:** hotels, residences, yachts, watches, dining, wellness.
- **Events:** Miami Art Week, Art Deco Weekend, Swim Week, the Boat Show, F1, festivals and launches.
- **Nightlife:** clubs, rooftops, live music, parties and the scene that wakes up after sunset.
- **Public events:** free fairs, art walks, parades, outdoor concerts. The Miami that belongs to everyone.
- **Culture and business:** art, fashion, music, real estate, economy and tech.

## How we work

1. **We show up.** Our team covers the city's most important events in person.
2. **We verify.** We use official sources and date every fact.
3. **We credit.** Photographers, models, stylists and venues are named.
4. **We are transparent.** Paid content is labeled "Partner". Fiction is labeled as fiction.
5. **We respect people.** We ask permission for portraits and honor every removal request. See our [Law, Intellectual Property & Permissions](/legal/intellectual-property.html) page.
