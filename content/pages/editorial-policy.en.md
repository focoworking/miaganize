## 1. Mission and independence

MIAGAZINE is a bilingual digital magazine about Miami published by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], a Florida limited liability company ("MIAGAZINE"), [MAILING ADDRESS], Miami, FL. Our mission is to show Miami through the lens: its culture, design, real estate, food, people and economy, told with accurate facts and strong images.

Editorial decisions are made by our editors alone. Advertisers, partners, sponsors, venues and sources do not approve, review or edit our independent journalism before publication. Buying an ad or a Partner package does not buy coverage, and declining to advertise never affects coverage.

Effective date: November 2, 2026.

## 2. Sourcing standards

- **Primary and official sources first.** We favor government records, court filings, official statistics, company filings and direct interviews. Journal articles list or link their sources.
- **Facts are dated.** Prices, rankings, statistics and "the largest / the newest" claims change. We state when a figure was current ("as of October 2026") and update evergreen guides periodically, noting the update date.
- **Attribution.** We say who said what. Anonymous sourcing is used only when information is important, cannot be obtained on the record, and the source would face real harm if named; at least one editor must know the source's identity.
- **No plagiarism.** We do not copy other outlets' work. When we build on another publication's reporting, we credit and link it.
- **Separation of news and opinion.** Opinion, reviews and editorials are labeled as such.

## 3. Speculative fiction and imagined scenarios

Some pieces imagine Miami's future or tell invented stories. These are clearly labeled as speculative fiction at the top of the article, and are not presented as reporting. Real people are not depicted saying or doing things they did not say or do.

## 4. Corrections and updates

We correct errors of fact quickly and transparently.

| Situation | What we do | Target timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Factual error (name, date, figure, attribution) | Fix the text and add a correction note | Within 2 business days of verification |
| Significant error affecting the story's meaning | Fix, add a prominent note at the top, and correct on social/newsletter where the error spread | As soon as verified |
| New information after publication | Update the article and add an "Updated" note with the date | When relevant |
| Typos, style, broken links | Fix without a note | Ongoing |

**Correction note format:** *"Correction (November 2, 2026): An earlier version of this article stated [the error]. In fact, [the correct fact]."* Notes appear at the end of the article (or at the top for significant errors) and in both language versions when both are affected.

We do not silently delete published articles to hide errors. Removal ("unpublishing") is reserved for exceptional cases, such as legal requirements, safety risks or serious privacy harm to private individuals.

To report an error, email corrections@miagazine.com with the URL, the statement you believe is wrong and supporting evidence.

## 5. Right of reply

People and organizations that are the subject of significant criticism in our reporting are given a reasonable opportunity to respond before publication. If you believe we treated you unfairly, you may request a reply at editorial@miagazine.com. We will consider publishing a relevant, concise response or adding your position to the article.

## 6. Alleged defamation: how to notify us

If you believe an article contains a false and defamatory statement of fact about you, send a written notice to legal@miagazine.com and by mail to [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [MAILING ADDRESS], Miami, FL. Please identify:

1. The article (title, URL, publication date).
2. The specific statements you allege are false and defamatory.
3. Why they are false, with any supporting evidence.
4. Your contact information.

Florida law requires a written pre-suit notice specifying the article and the statements alleged to be false and defamatory, served at least five days before a civil action for libel or slander against a publication in a newspaper, periodical or other medium ([Fla. Stat. §770.01](http://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0770/Sections/0770.01.html)). Whether or not the statute applies in a particular case, we treat every such notice seriously: we review it promptly with our editors and counsel, and if we find an error we publish a full and fair correction as described in section 4.

## 7. Sponsored content, advertising and affiliate links

### 7.1 Partner content

Content paid for or supplied by a brand is labeled **"Partner"** at the top of the piece, in listings and in social posts, and names the sponsor. Partner content is visually distinguished from editorial content and is not written to look like independent reporting. Our editorial staff does not write reviews of a partner's products as part of a paid package.

### 7.2 FTC compliance

Under Section 5 of the FTC Act ([15 U.S.C. §45](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/45)), advertising must not be deceptive, including content that hides its commercial nature. The FTC's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising ([16 CFR Part 255](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255)) require clear and conspicuous disclosure of any material connection between an endorser and a brand (payment, free products, family or business ties). See the FTC's guidance at [ftc.gov](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews).

### 7.3 Creators and social media

When MIAGAZINE or creators working with us publish sponsored posts:

- The disclosure is in the post itself, visible without clicking "more," and in the language of the post: **#ad**, **#sponsored**, "Partner" or "Paid partnership with [Brand]."
- Platform tools (such as Instagram's "Paid partnership" label) are used in addition to, not instead of, a clear disclosure when needed.
- Video sponsorships are disclosed in the video (spoken and on screen), not only in the description.
- Disclosures are not hidden among hashtags or at the end of long captions.
- Creators must only share honest opinions and real experiences.

### 7.4 Affiliate links

Some articles may include affiliate links that earn MIAGAZINE a commission if you buy. We disclose this near the top of the article ("MIAGAZINE may earn a commission from links in this article") and affiliate relationships never determine our recommendations.

Advertising inquiries: partners@miagazine.com.

## 8. Gifts, press trips and comps

- We may accept press invitations, review meals, hotel stays or event access when needed to report a story. When coverage results from complimentary access, we disclose it in the article.
- We do not accept cash, gifts of significant value or anything conditioned on favorable coverage. Items of more than nominal value received for review are returned, donated or disclosed.
- Accepting access does not guarantee coverage or a positive review.

## 9. Conflicts of interest

Editors, writers and contributors must disclose any personal, financial or family relationship with the subjects of their work. People do not cover businesses they own, invest in, or are paid by. Where a conflict cannot be avoided, it is disclosed to readers. Staff may not use non-public information obtained through reporting for personal gain (for example, in real estate or investments).

## 10. Use of artificial intelligence

- AI tools may support translation, transcription, metadata, headline suggestions and research organization. **A human editor reviews and is accountable for everything we publish.**
- Where AI materially assisted the writing of a piece, we disclose it.
- We **never** publish AI-generated images as editorial photography or present synthetic images as real events.
- We do not enter confidential source material or unpublished contributor work into AI tools that use it for training.
- Our rules on others using our content for AI are on the [Law, Intellectual Property & Permissions](/legal/intellectual-property.html) page.

## 11. Photo ethics

- Editorial photographs must not be manipulated in ways that mislead: no adding, removing or moving elements that change what happened.
- Standard adjustments (exposure, color, contrast, cropping, dust removal) are acceptable if they keep the photo truthful.
- Fashion and portrait editorials may include retouching; when retouching goes beyond standard adjustments, the credit notes it (e.g., "Retouched image").
- Composites, illustrations and photo-illustrations are labeled.
- Captions identify people, places and dates accurately. Staged or recreated scenes are labeled.

## 12. Protecting sources

We protect the identity of confidential sources and do not reveal it without the source's permission, subject to legal obligations. We store sensitive material securely and limit internal access. Readers who share tips should know that standard email is not fully secure; ask editorial@miagazine.com for safer options.

## 13. Event coverage ethics

- We photograph public events openly, identify ourselves when asked, and respect requests not to be photographed.
- We follow organizer rules for private events and do not enter areas without access.
- We avoid publishing images that humiliate people, show them in vulnerable moments without news value, or identify minors without parental consent.
- Removal requests for event photos are handled as described on the [Law, Intellectual Property & Permissions](/legal/intellectual-property.html) page.

## 14. Newsletter compliance

"The Lens" is sent only to people who subscribe. Under the CAN-SPAM Act ([15 U.S.C. §7701 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/chapter-103)) and the FTC's rule ([16 CFR Part 316](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-316)), every commercial message we send:

- Uses accurate "From" information and non-deceptive subject lines.
- Identifies sponsored messages or sections as advertising.
- Includes our valid physical postal address: [MAILING ADDRESS], Miami, FL.
- Includes a working one-click unsubscribe link; opt-outs are honored within 10 business days (in practice, immediately).

## 15. Accessibility

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines ([WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)) Level AA: descriptive alt text for photos, captions for video where feasible, sufficient color contrast, keyboard navigation and readable layouts in both languages. If something is not accessible to you, email editorial@miagazine.com and we will provide the content in another way.
