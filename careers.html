Why work with MIAGAZINE

We are building Miami's visual luxury magazine from the ground up, with a small and demanding team. Here your work gets published, carries your name and reaches the brands, venues and readers that matter. We look for people with taste, reliability and the drive to be where things happen.

What we look for

  • A portfolio or recent work samples (links or PDF).
  • Availability for events across Miami-Dade and Broward, including nights and weekends.
  • Professional English and/or Spanish (both is a plus).
  • Respect for people: image releases, credits and professional conduct.
  • Your own gear (for photo and video).

How to apply — by appointment

  1. Book an appointment with the "Book an appointment" button (virtual or in person in Miami).
  2. Send your portfolio to careers@miagazine.com with the subject: Position – [role] – [your name].
  3. Interview of 20–30 minutes. For photo and video we may ask for a paid test or a trial coverage.
  4. Answer within 10 business days.

Interviews are by appointment only. Out of respect for your time and ours, we don't take walk-ins.

For models and talent

Castings for editorials and events are also by appointment only. We never charge to attend a casting or to appear in the magazine. If anyone asks you for money on behalf of MIAGAZINE, report it to legal@miagazine.com. Anyone under 18 may participate only with written permission from a parent or legal guardian.

Equal opportunity

MIAGAZINE values Miami's diversity. We evaluate every person on talent and work, without discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability or any other status protected by law.

Notice

Applying or interviewing does not create an employment relationship or a contract. Every collaboration is formalized in writing. Data you send us is handled under our Privacy Policy.
