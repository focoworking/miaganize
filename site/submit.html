What you get

  • Publication on miagazine.com, in The Lens newsletter and on our social channels
  • Full credits for your team and a photographer profile linked to your portfolio
  • A share kit and a “Published in MIAGAZINE” badge
  • 20% commission on any brand or venue you refer that becomes a partner
  • Priority for paid assignments and invitations to our events

Guidelines

  • 8–20 images, minimum 3000px long side, sRGB JPG, no watermarks
  • Unpublished in other magazines (your own social is fine)
  • Shot in Miami or South Florida, or with a clear Miami connection
  • Title, 80–150 words of story, and complete credits
  • Model and location releases where applicable
  • Real photography only — no generative AI imagery

Rights

You keep your copyright. By submitting, you grant MIAGAZINE a non-exclusive license to publish your images with credit on our website, newsletter, social channels, quarterly issues and promotion. Any commercial use by a third-party brand requires your separate agreement and payment.
