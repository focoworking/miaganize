Miami Art Week 2026: The Guide

Miami Art Week 2026 runs from November 30 to December 6. Art Basel Miami Beach opens to the public December 4–6 at the Miami Beach Convention Center, with VIP previews December 2–3. Satellite fairs, gallery openings and private dinners fill Miami Beach, Wynwood and the Design District all week.

Which neighborhoods should you plan around?

Miami Beach (Art Basel, beachfront fairs and hotel activations), Wynwood (galleries and murals), Design District (luxury brand activations and collections) and downtown/Edgewater (museums and waterfront).

How do you get around?

Group your days by neighborhood. Ride-share and walking within each area beat driving between them, especially on the causeways in the evening.

FAQ

When is Miami Art Week 2026?

Miami Art Week 2026 runs from November 30 to December 6, 2026. Art Basel Miami Beach is open to the public December 4–6, 2026, at the Miami Beach Convention Center, with VIP preview days on December 2–3.

Where is Art Basel Miami Beach held?

Art Basel Miami Beach takes place at the Miami Beach Convention Center, 1901 Convention Center Drive, Miami Beach, Florida.

What is the best way to get around during Miami Art Week?

Plan by neighborhood: Miami Beach for Art Basel and beach fairs, Wynwood and the Design District for galleries and satellite events. Traffic is heavy, so ride-share, the free Miami Beach trolley and walking within each area save the most time.
